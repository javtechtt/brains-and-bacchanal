# Brains & Bacchanal — Player / Host Reference

This is a concise reference for UI wording and Host controls.

## Goal

Start with 1,000 BB.

BB is both:
- money,
- score.

Highest BB after Round 4 wins unless tied leaders go to Sudden Death.

## Game Flow

1. Round 1 — Nah, That Too Easy!
2. Market
3. Round 2 — Shake Up Yuhself!
4. Market
5. Round 3 — Think Fast!
6. Market
7. Round 4 — What Yuh Think About This?
8. Sudden Death if necessary

## Phones

Phones are controllers.

There is **no digital buzzer before Family Feud**.

## Bacchanal Cards

Each team starts with:
- one Disruption,
- one Power / Strategy,
- one Recovery / Social.

Maximum one Bacchanal Card per team per challenge.

Clash response window: 3 seconds.

Category rule:
- Disruption beats Power,
- Power beats Recovery,
- Recovery beats Disruption.

Tie → **PART DAT FIGHT!**

## Market

Opens before Rounds 2, 3 and 4.

Teams spend BB to buy advantages.

One copy of each item per Market visit.

Items expire after the immediately following round.

## Maco Mail

Mystery outcome:
- money,
- penalty,
- advantage,
- sabotage,
- game-changing effect.

Draws without replacement.

## Round 1 — Nah, That Too Easy!

- 15 questions: 5 Easy, 5 Medium, 5 Hard,
- **every team answers the same question at the same time**,
- 60 seconds per question,
- no buzzer,
- each team nominates one player for Easy, one for Medium, one for Hard, and
  only that player submits for their difficulty,
- the correct answer is revealed after the retry flow finishes.

| Difficulty | Value |
|---|---:|
| Easy | 20 |
| Medium | 30 |
| Hard | 50 |

Wrong answers score 0 — nothing is deducted. The value counts as BB **and** as
separate Round 1 points, which decide the Round 1 winner.

Cards you can play: **Maco!**, **Double It!**, **ALLYUH HELP ME!**,
**FORGIVE MEH!** — not Steups!, Gimme Dat! or Doh Know.

The FORGIVE MEH! retry-window duration is not decided yet.

## Round 2

Host runs physical game.

Host selects winner.

Winner receives 500 BB.

## Round 3 — how it is won

Four challenges: Think Fast, Guess the Logo, All Answers Begin With, Sing a Song.

Winning a challenge gives your team **one Round 3 win**. Most Round 3 wins takes
the round. If that ties, the tied teams play **actual rock-paper-scissors** —
not a Bacchanal Clash, and no card is spent.

BB is separate: only Think Fast and Sing a Song pay BB (500 each). Winning
Round 3 overall pays none.

The **game** supplies every topic, logo, letter, prompt and song scenario. The
Host runs the challenge and judges; the Host does not make up the content.

## Think Fast

- no buzzer,
- turn-based, and the team that **won the previous round goes first**,
- Host judges each answer,
- a team that cannot answer is out of this challenge,
- the last team still answering wins **500 BB** and the Round 3 win.

## Guess the Logo

- no buzzer,
- 10 seconds per logo,
- teams shout; the Host decides who was first and whether it is right,
- a wrong answer does not stop the others in that window,
- no correct answer → next logo,
- each correct logo = **1 challenge point**; **first to 5** is the normal target,
- the **Host confirms the winner** and may end it sooner or let it run longer,
- winner takes the Round 3 win. No BB.

## All Answers Begin With...

- the game supplies the letter and the prompts,
- every valid answer starts with that letter,
- 10 seconds per prompt,
- first valid answer = **1 challenge point**; **first to 5** is the normal target,
- the **Host confirms the winner**, sooner or later as they judge,
- winner takes the Round 3 win. No BB.

## Sing a Song

- the game supplies a scenario or an image,
- 10 seconds per item,
- first team to sing a **full line** that matches scores **1 challenge point**,
- the Host decides who was first and whether it counts,
- **first to 3** is the normal target,
- the **Host confirms the winner**, sooner or later,
- winner takes **500 BB** and the Round 3 win.

## Family Feud

First digital buzzer section.

Includes:
- face-off,
- board answers,
- strikes,
- control,
- steal,
- optional steal wager.

Q4/Q5 are already doubled.

## Three-Team Family Feud

- 2nd vs 3rd first,
- first two questions,
- loser keeps BB but cannot earn more Family Feud BB,
- winner faces entering 1st place,
- remaining questions are played,
- tie after first two → entering 2nd advances.

## Sudden Death

- first to two consecutive correct answers wins,
- wrong answer after buzz = immediate loss,
- buzz + timeout = immediate loss,
- no buzz = no penalty, next question,
- no powers/items/wagers.

## Host Authority

Host decides:
- subjective answer validity,
- who spoke first when human judgment is required,
- physical/creative winners.

Server decides:
- BB,
- timers,
- legal actions,
- cards,
- Market,
- Maco Mail,
- wagers,
- buzzer acceptance,
- pause/resume.

Active-player disconnect:
- game pauses automatically,
- only Host resumes.
